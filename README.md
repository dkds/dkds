# Hi there! 👋 I’m Dakshitha

### Tech Explorer | PC Gamer | Street Photographer | Time & Space Traveler (without a TARDIS)

By day, I’m a professional software engineer with over eight years of experience working with industrial software systems, mainly using the microservices architecture.

By night, I dive into random tech rabbit holes, specially on history of tech (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/technologyconnections" target="_blank">Technology Connections</a> 🗄️, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@LGR" target="_blank">LGR</a> 🖥️ and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@Techmoan" target="_blank">Techmoan</a> 💽 fan here 🚀) and moonlight as a gamer (pcmasterrace all the way 💻!).

I mostly work with **Java** and **JavaScript**, among others, though after all, programming languages are just tools for building. What I’m truly passionate about is **software architecture** and **system integrations**.  
It is quite satisfying to see complex architecture taking shape by fitting various technologies together.

Besides coding, I am a **hobbyist street photographer** and a **traveler**. People and our shared history fascinates me, and with my camera I get to capture and freeze some moments in time before they slip into the past. A bit of time travelling of my own!  
📸 (still working on photography and editing skills, but some of my work so far is at <a href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/dakshithad/" target="_blank">flickr.com</a>)

- 🔐 **Privacy-focused** and a **human-centered tech advocate**
- 🐧 Lifelong **Linux enthusiast** & **open-source believer**

- 🌍 Based in **Sri Lanka**
- 🧠 **Currently learning:** Exploring latest spring framework features, refreshing on event-driven architecture with Kafka.
- 🤝 Looking to collaborate on open-source projects in my free time, particularly those focused on **privacy**, **human-centered tech**.
